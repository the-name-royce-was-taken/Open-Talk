Frequently Asked Questions

Is opentalk a free Omegle alternative?

Yes. opentalk is a 100% free Omegle alternative — random video and text chat with strangers, no signup required.

Why did Omegle shut down?

Omegle shut down in November 2023 after years of moderation issues. opentalk is built as a safer modern replacement with active moderation and an instant report system.

Is opentalk like Monkey app or OmeTV?

Yes. Like Monkey app and OmeTV, opentalk pairs you instantly with random strangers — but it works in any browser, no download needed.

How is opentalk different from Omegle?

opentalk has active moderation, an instant report button, and auto-bans for abusive users — the things Omegle was missing.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. It runs in any modern mobile browser, and opentalk is also available on the Google Play Store as a native app.

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Start and you're matched with a random stranger instantly — fully anonymous.

Is opentalk safe to use?

opentalk is designed for users 18 and older. Every chat session has an instant one-tap report button. Reported users are automatically reviewed and banned within minutes. It is the most actively moderated free random chat site available.

How does opentalk work?

opentalk uses WebRTC — the same peer-to-peer technology powering Google Meet and Zoom — for live video and audio. No servers store your video stream. Open opentalk.club, click Start Video Chat, and within seconds you are live with a random stranger from anywhere in the world.
